How it works

From upload to insight in seconds

A three-stage pipeline processes your resume through text extraction, entity recognition, and role-based matching to produce a structured fit report.

01
Upload & Parse

Drop your PDF, DOCX, or TXT resume. The preprocessing pipeline extracts raw text using PyMuPDF and python-docx with multi-engine fallback.

02
NER Extraction

A custom-trained CRF NER model identifies 9 entity classes — EXP, EDU, SKILL, DATE, PROJ, PER, ORG, LOC, LANG — directly from your resume tokens.

03
Role Matching

Cosine similarity measures text-to-job-profile alignment. A ML quality classifier and NER-derived experience score combine into a weighted final score.

04
Actionable Report

You receive a detailed breakdown: component scores, matched and missing skills, completeness flags, level fit, and a prioritized improvement plan.

Features

Everything you need to stand out

Six specialized analysis modules working together to give you a complete picture of your resume's strengths and gaps.

Role Fit Scoring

A composite score from three engines: text similarity (50%), ML quality classifier (30%), and CRF NER experience score (20%) — calibrated for IT roles.

Core
Skill Gap Detection

Compares your detected skills against curated role profiles. Shows exactly which required skills are present and which are missing — as chips.

Precision
Experience Extraction

The CRF NER model identifies DATE entities within EXP sections to calculate years of professional experience — excluding education dates to avoid overcounting.

NER-Powered
Section Completeness

Checks five key resume sections — Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects — and flags any that are missing or underdeveloped.

Structure
Level Recommendation

Fits your profile against Entry, Mid, and Senior levels simultaneously and recommends the optimal level based on experience and skill depth.

Smart Fit
Prioritized Recommendations

Every gap becomes an action item — categorized as High, Medium, or Info priority — so you know exactly what to fix first to maximize your fit score.
